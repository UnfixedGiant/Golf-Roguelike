# Golf Roguelike

Idea Origin:
Watching caedrals stream where he was watching the lck and played a shitty golf game in between lck games.(T1 vs DK)


Top down golf game
Golf ball that has to reach different holes around the map which are randomly generated.
Once the player reaches a hole, they are rewarded with a favor upgrade.(Permanent upgrade for the run)


Map:
The map will be generated randomly using a range of different tiles up until the player reaches 18 holes.
Once the player reaches 18 holes, they will have the option of playing 5 more holes at a harder difficulty and will be rewarded with extra gold.


Currency:
Gold will be used for permanent upgrades for the player before they start a run.
This will be items that the player can have. e.g(a hat, a trail)


Permanent upgrades:
Ball trail which damages surrounding areas.
ball material which has gives different attributes to the ball.


Enemies:
Golf Tees:
These enemies will simply just dash towards the player ball when they are close. They will damage the player on contact.


Flag enemies:
Wind flags will be able to blow the player away and damage them.
Laser flags will be able to damage the player from a range.

Bosses:
The Golf Cart - The first boss.
Abilities and playstyle still TBA

Blessings:
The player will be able to choose 3 total blessings at which point they will only be able to upgrade these for the rest of the run

Fire - Infernal Spinner - Gives the player fire upgrades
